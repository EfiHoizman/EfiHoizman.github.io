Telegram Personal Assistant

Your calendar, tasks and reminders.
In one private conversation.

TelegramHelper is a personal application operated for its owner's use. It connects a private Telegram conversation to Google Calendar and Google Tasks.

01 / CALENDAR

See and manage meetings

View today's meetings, upcoming events and next week's schedule from the connected account's primary Google calendar. Create, reschedule or cancel events after confirmation, including events originally created outside Telegram.

02 / TASKS

Keep tasks in sync

Create and manage tasks through Telegram, with two-way synchronization between local task records and Google Tasks. Tasks can have dates; Google Tasks dates do not provide reminder times.

03 / REMINDERS

Remember what matters

Use one-time and recurring reminders. Event-linked reminders check the original Google Calendar event before delivery. Local reminder state is retained so failed checks can be retried.

04 / DAILY OVERVIEW

Start with your schedule

Receive a summary of meetings and open tasks. Use supported commands, inline buttons, Hebrew messages or voice messages, which are transcribed and interpreted using Google's Gemini API.

A personal application

The bot is limited to one configured Telegram user. This information site does not provide public registration, Google sign-in or access to the bot. Google authorization is completed separately using a Desktop OAuth client.

The application is independently operated and is not an official Google or Telegram product. Read the privacy policy and terms of use before authorizing access.
