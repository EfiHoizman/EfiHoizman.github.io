Purpose and access

TelegramHelper is an independently operated personal assistant for one configured Telegram user. It is not a public subscription service. This website provides information and does not grant access to the bot or initiate Google authorization.

Authorization and confirmed actions

Only connect Google data you are authorized to manage. Review proposed event and task changes before confirming them. Natural-language interpretation and voice transcription can be mistaken. Google authorization permits API access; it does not guarantee that every requested operation will be available.

Availability and limitations

Messages, synchronization and reminder delivery depend on Telegram, Google APIs, network connectivity and the server. They can be delayed or fail. Retries may occur, and the application does not promise uninterrupted availability, immediate synchronization or exactly-once reminder delivery.

Check important dates and changes in Google Calendar and Google Tasks. The assistant is not an emergency service and should not be the sole mechanism for a critical deadline.

Data and account controls

The privacy policy describes accessed information, storage, providers and retention. Google access can be revoked through the user's account. Revoking access does not automatically delete local records or backups. Do not send passwords, API keys or OAuth tokens in Telegram messages.

Changes and contact

The operator may maintain or discontinue this private application. Changes to data access or purposes must be disclosed before they are introduced. Google and Telegram names identify integrations and do not imply endorsement.
